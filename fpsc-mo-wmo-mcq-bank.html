According to FPSC's published syllabus for BS-16/17 posts, the written test has 100 MCQs: Part I English (20 marks) and Part II Professional test (80 marks), with negative marking of 0.25 for each wrong answer. Always confirm the exact pattern in your own FPSC advertisement.
