Yes. It covers the full FPSC MO/WMO syllabus along with past papers and timed mock tests, so you can prepare completely in one place.

What is the FPSC MO/WMO test pattern?

The FPSC MO/WMO written test for BS-17 posts has 100 MCQs. English carries 20 marks, and the remaining 80 marks are distributed among the core MBBS subjects, Care vs Cure, Health Policies of Govt and Major Challenges for the Health Sector. Each wrong answer carries negative marking of 0.25.

How can I get the past papers?

You can start studying them right away. They are part of the FPSC MO/WMO subscription.
