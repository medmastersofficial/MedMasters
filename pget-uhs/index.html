One subscription, all devices, with progress synced across sessions. What Is PGET? The Postgraduate Entrance Test (PGET) is conducted by the University of Health Sciences (UHS), Lahore, on behalf of the Government of Punjab's Specialized Healthcare & Medical Education Department, under the Central Induction Policy (CIP). Passing PGET is mandatory for admission to Level-III postgraduate medical and dental programmes — MD, MS, MDS and FCPS — for the January 2027 intake. Passing the test alone does not guarantee admission; candidates remain subject to eligibility requirements, merit criteria, and seat availability. PGET 2026 will be held on Sunday, 25 October 2026, simultaneously at Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan and Rawalpindi. PGET Exam Format Quick reference for what to expect on exam day. Always cross-check the latest details on the official UHS admissions portal. Total MCQs 200 (100 per paper) Papers Paper 1 + Paper 2 Duration 2 hours per paper Passing Criteria 75 marks per paper Test Centres Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan, Rawalpindi Question Type MCQs / Best-Choice Questions Paper 1: Basic Sciences (10 AM – 12 Noon) Paper 1 draws from the MBBS/BDS curriculum and is set separately for medical and dental graduates. It forms the common foundation paper every PGET candidate sits. Paper 2: Specialty-Specific (1 PM – 3 PM) Paper 2 changes based on the chosen specialty at registration — one of the 16 PGET categories. No change of specialty is permitted after final submission of the application. Who Can Apply? PGET eligibility is straightforward, but every requirement must be met before applying through the UHS admissions portal. Educational qualification: MBBS or BDS degree from an institution recognized by the Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PM&DC). House job / internship: Successful completion of a one-year house job in a hospital recognized by PM&DC. Registration: Valid permanent PM&DC registration. PGET result: A pass result in PGET (minimum 75 marks per paper) is required for induction. Age limit: Under 40 years on the last date for submission of the application. Domicile: Punjab domicile is required for open merit and disability quota seats. Foreign candidates: Must possess NOC/Study Visa from the Ministry of Interior, Government of Pakistan, and NOC from the Home Department. Always confirm the latest eligibility and application steps on the official UHS PGET portal before applying. PGET Score 75% of merit MBBS/BDS Aggregate 15 points University Exam Positions 5 points House Job 5 points The PGET result carries 75% weightage in the calculation of the final admission merit score for Level-III induction. The remaining 25% is built from: MBBS/BDS aggregate marks — up to 15 points. Position in university-level professional examinations — up to 5 points. Candidates earn 1 mark per position for MBBS graduates and 1.25 marks per position for BDS graduates, for 1st, 2nd and 3rd positions in each professional examination conducted at the university level (not college level). House job — up to 5 points, scored on where the house job was completed relative to the candidate's parent institution and province. Because PGET dominates the merit formula, a strong PGET score is by far the single biggest lever a candidate controls.
