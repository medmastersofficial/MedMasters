What Is PGET?

The Postgraduate Entrance Test (PGET) is conducted by the University of Health Sciences (UHS), Lahore, on behalf of the Government of Punjab's Specialized Healthcare & Medical Education Department, under the Central Induction Policy (CIP).

Passing PGET is mandatory for admission to Level-III postgraduate medical and dental programmes (MD, MS, MDS and FCPS) for the January 2027 intake. Passing the test alone does not guarantee admission; candidates remain subject to eligibility requirements, merit criteria, and seat availability.

PGET 2026 will be held on Sunday, 25 October 2026, simultaneously at Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan and Rawalpindi.

Paper 1: Basic Sciences (10 AM – 12 Noon)

Paper 1 draws from the MBBS/BDS curriculum and is set separately for medical and dental graduates. It forms the common foundation paper every PGET candidate sits.

Paper 2: Specialty-Specific (1 PM – 3 PM)

Paper 2 changes based on the chosen specialty at registration, one of the 16 PGET categories. No change of specialty is permitted after final submission of the application.

Who Can Apply?

PGET eligibility is straightforward, but every requirement must be met before applying through the UHS admissions portal.

  • Educational qualification: MBBS or BDS degree from an institution recognized by the Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PM&DC).
  • House job / internship: Successful completion of a one-year house job in a hospital recognized by PM&DC.
  • Registration: Valid permanent PM&DC registration.
  • PGET result: A pass result in PGET (minimum 75 marks per paper) is required for induction.
  • Age limit: Under 40 years on the last date for submission of the application.
  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required for open merit and disability quota seats.
  • Foreign candidates: Must possess NOC/Study Visa from the Ministry of Interior, Government of Pakistan, and NOC from the Home Department.

Always confirm the latest eligibility and application steps on the official UHS PGET portal before applying.

PGET Admission Weightage

How the PGET score feeds into your final Level-III induction merit for MD/MS/MDS/FCPS.

PGET Score
75% of merit
MBBS/BDS Aggregate
15 points
University Exam Positions
5 points
House Job
5 points

The PGET result carries 75% weightage in the calculation of the final admission merit score for Level-III induction. The remaining 25% is built from:

  • MBBS/BDS aggregate marks: up to 15 points.
  • Position in university-level professional examinations: up to 5 points. Candidates earn 1 mark per position for MBBS graduates and 1.25 marks per position for BDS graduates, for 1st, 2nd and 3rd positions in each professional examination conducted at the university level (not college level).
  • House job: up to 5 points, scored on where the house job was completed relative to the candidate's parent institution and province.

Because PGET dominates the merit formula, a strong PGET score is by far the single biggest lever a candidate controls.

How to Ace PGET

Seven high-yield strategies that consistently separate successful candidates from re-takers.

1. Understand the exam architecture first

Before opening a textbook, internalize the structure: two papers, 200 MCQs total, 75 marks required per paper. Knowing that PGET carries 75% of your final induction merit changes how seriously every mark is treated.

2. Cover the basic sciences systematically

Paper 1 draws directly from the MBBS/BDS curriculum. Move system by system through Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology and Community Medicine rather than chapter by chapter at random.

3. Practice MCQs daily

Reading alone does not predict PGET performance. Active recall does. Solve MCQs from day one, alongside reading. MedMasters' specialty-wise MCQ bank allows subject-wise practice from the first day.

4. Master your Paper 2 specialty early

Since specialty selection is locked at registration, start Paper 2 preparation for your chosen category (whether Medicine and Allied, a Surgery group, or a BDS specialty) well before the final weeks.

5. Use a smart practice platform with tracking

A platform that tracks accuracy per subject saves enormous time. MedMasters' dashboard does this automatically with colour-coded accuracy rings, subject-wise progress, and confidence levels.

6. Take timed mock exams in the final stretch

In the last 3 to 4 weeks, simulate exam conditions: 100 MCQs, 2 hours, no breaks, for both Paper 1 and Paper 2. This trains pace and exposes time-management gaps before 25 October.

7. Track every point of the 25% too

Since aggregate marks, university exam positions, and house job placement collectively make up 25% of the merit score, keep documentation ready early: it removes last-minute administrative stress around the PGET date.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PGET UHS?

PGET is the Postgraduate Entrance Test conducted by the University of Health Sciences (UHS), Lahore, on behalf of the Government of Punjab. Passing it is mandatory for admission to Level-III postgraduate medical and dental programmes (MD, MS, MDS, FCPS) under the Central Induction Policy.

What is the PGET exam pattern?

Two papers of 100 marks each: Paper 1 (basic sciences, 2 hours, 10 AM–12 Noon) and Paper 2 (specialty-specific, 2 hours, 1 PM–3 PM). The minimum passing score is 75 marks per paper.

How many specialties does PGET offer?

16 in total: 11 for MBBS graduates (including Surgery & Allied Group I and Group II) and 5 for BDS graduates (Prosthodontics, Operative Dentistry, Periodontology, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Orthodontics).

What weightage does PGET carry in Level-III induction?

PGET carries 75% weightage in the final admission merit score. The remaining 25% comes from MBBS/BDS aggregate marks (15 points), university-level professional exam positions (5 points), and house job (5 points).

When is PGET 2026 and how do I register?

PGET 2026 is on Sunday, 25 October 2026, at Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan and Rawalpindi. Online registration runs 23 August–22 September 2026 via the UHS admissions portal, with a non-refundable Rs. 10,000 processing fee.

Does passing PGET guarantee admission?

No. Passing PGET is mandatory but not sufficient: candidates remain subject to eligibility requirements, the full merit criteria, and seat availability in their chosen specialty and category.
