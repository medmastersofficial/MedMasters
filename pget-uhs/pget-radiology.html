About PGET UHS Radiology

PGET UHS Radiology is one of the 16 specialty categories offered under the Postgraduate Entrance Test (PGET), conducted by the University of Health Sciences (UHS), Lahore. Passing PGET is mandatory for MBBS graduates seeking Level-III postgraduate induction (MD, MS, MDS or FCPS) under the Punjab Central Induction Policy, for the January 2027 intake.

Candidates registering for Radiology sit Paper 1 (common MBBS basic sciences, 100 MCQs, 2 hours) and Paper 2 (specialty-specific content for Radiology, 100 MCQs, 2 hours). A minimum of 75 marks per paper is required to pass.
