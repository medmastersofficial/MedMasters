About FCPS Part 1 Otorhinolaryngology (ENT)

Complete syllabus, paper format, eligibility, and high-yield content areas for the FCPS Part 1 Otorhinolaryngology (ENT) examination.

What Is FCPS Part 1 Otorhinolaryngology (ENT)?

FCPS Part 1 in Otorhinolaryngology (ENT) is the entry-level qualifying examination for candidates seeking residency training in Otorhinolaryngology and Head & Neck Surgery. It is conducted by the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (CPSP) and assesses foundational knowledge before progression to FCPS Part 2 and specialty training.

Passing FCPS Part 1 in ENT is a prerequisite for joining accredited postgraduate training programmes at CPSP-recognized hospitals.

Paper 1 Syllabus

Paper 1 is the common basic sciences paper, almost identical across most FCPS Part 1 specialties. It covers the foundational subjects every medical graduate has studied:

  • Anatomy: Gross Anatomy, Embryology, Histology, Neuroanatomy
  • Physiology: System-wise physiology covering all body systems
  • Biochemistry: Metabolism, Molecular Biology, Clinical Correlations
  • Pharmacology: General principles, autonomic system, system-based pharmacology
  • Pathology: General and Systemic Pathology
  • Microbiology: Bacteriology, Virology, Parasitology, Mycology, Immunology
  • Community Medicine: Biostatistics, Epidemiology, Public Health
  • Behavioural Sciences: Doctor-patient communication, ethics, psychology

Paper 2 Syllabus

Paper 2 is specialty-specific and tests applied basic sciences relevant to Otorhinolaryngology (ENT). The major topics covered include:

  • Ear Anatomy & Physiology: External, middle and inner ear structure and function
  • Hearing & Balance Disorders: Conductive and sensorineural hearing loss, vertigo, vestibular conditions
  • Otology & Otitis: Otitis externa, otitis media, cholesteatoma, mastoiditis
  • Nose & Paranasal Sinuses: Anatomy, rhinitis, sinusitis, nasal polyps, epistaxis
  • Throat & Pharynx: Pharyngitis, tonsillitis, adenoiditis, pharyngeal abscesses
  • Larynx & Voice: Laryngitis, vocal cord lesions, laryngeal carcinoma
  • Head & Neck Lesions: Neck masses, salivary gland disorders, thyroid surgery basics
  • ENT Pharmacology: Drugs used in ENT practice

Who Can Apply?

  • Educational qualification: MBBS or BDS degree recognized by the Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PMDC) or equivalent authority.
  • House job: Completion of a one-year house job in a PMDC-recognized institution.
  • Registration: Valid PMDC/PMC registration certificate.
  • International graduates: Foreign medical degrees must be verified and equated by PMDC.

Always confirm latest eligibility and application steps on the official CPSP website.
