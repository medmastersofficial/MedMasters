FCPS Part 1 Obstetrics & Gynaecology at a Glance

Obstetrics & Gynaecology is the entry point for residency training in Obstetrics, Gynaecology, and Reproductive Medicine.

Candidates sit two papers. Paper 1 covers common basic sciences (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology, Pathology, Microbiology, Community Medicine, Behavioural Sciences). Paper 2 focuses on gynae & obs-specific basic sciences. Both papers must be passed individually with at least 75%.
