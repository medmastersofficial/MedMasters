FCPS Part 1 Medicine & Allied at a Glance

Medicine & Allied is the most popular FCPS Part 1 specialty and the entry point for residency in Internal Medicine and its allied disciplines, including Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Pulmonology, Endocrinology, Nephrology, Neurology, Rheumatology, Haematology, Infectious Diseases, Dermatology, Paediatrics, and Hepatology.

Candidates sit two papers. Paper 1 covers common basic sciences (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology, Pathology, Microbiology, Community Medicine, Behavioural Sciences). Paper 2 focuses on systemic medicine across the body systems listed above. Both papers must be passed individually with at least 75%.
