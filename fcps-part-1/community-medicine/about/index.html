About FCPS Part 1 Community Medicine

Complete syllabus, paper format, eligibility, and high-yield content areas for the FCPS Part 1 Community Medicine examination.

What Is FCPS Part 1 Community Medicine?

FCPS Part 1 in Community Medicine is the entry-level qualifying examination for candidates seeking residency training in Community Medicine, Public Health, and Preventive Medicine. It is conducted by the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (CPSP) and assesses foundational knowledge before progression to FCPS Part 2 and specialty training.

Passing FCPS Part 1 in Community Medicine is a prerequisite for joining accredited postgraduate training programmes at CPSP-recognized hospitals.

Paper 1 Syllabus

Paper 1 is the common basic sciences paper, almost identical across most FCPS Part 1 specialties. It covers the foundational subjects every medical graduate has studied:

  • Anatomy: Gross Anatomy, Embryology, Histology, Neuroanatomy
  • Physiology: System-wise physiology covering all body systems
  • Biochemistry: Metabolism, Molecular Biology, Clinical Correlations
  • Pharmacology: General principles, autonomic system, system-based pharmacology
  • Pathology: General and Systemic Pathology
  • Microbiology: Bacteriology, Virology, Parasitology, Mycology, Immunology
  • Community Medicine: Biostatistics, Epidemiology, Public Health
  • Behavioural Sciences: Doctor-patient communication, ethics, psychology

Paper 2 Syllabus

Paper 2 is specialty-specific and tests applied basic sciences relevant to Community Medicine. The major topics covered include:

  • Epidemiology: Study designs, measures of disease frequency, association and causation
  • Biostatistics: Descriptive and inferential statistics, hypothesis testing
  • Disease Surveillance: Notifiable diseases, outbreak investigation, surveillance systems
  • Vaccination & Immunisation: EPI schedule, cold chain, vaccine pharmacology
  • Nutrition & Health: Macronutrients, micronutrients, malnutrition, nutritional assessment
  • Maternal & Child Health: Antenatal care, family planning, IMNCI
  • Environmental & Occupational Health: Water, sanitation, air pollution, occupational hazards
  • Health Systems & Policy: Healthcare delivery, primary healthcare, SDGs

Who Can Apply?

  • Educational qualification: MBBS or BDS degree recognized by the Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PMDC) or equivalent authority.
  • House job: Completion of a one-year house job in a PMDC-recognized institution.
  • Registration: Valid PMDC/PMC registration certificate.
  • International graduates: Foreign medical degrees must be verified and equated by PMDC.

Always confirm latest eligibility and application steps on the official CPSP website.
