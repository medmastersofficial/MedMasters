FCPS Part 1 Surgery & Allied at a Glance

Surgery & Allied is the entry point for residency training in General Surgery and its allied disciplines, including Orthopaedics, Cardiothoracic Surgery, Neurosurgery, Urology, Plastic Surgery, and Paediatric Surgery.

Candidates sit two papers. Paper 1 covers common basic sciences (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology, Pathology, Microbiology, Community Medicine, Behavioural Sciences). Paper 2 focuses on surgery topics across the systems listed above. Both papers must be passed individually with at least 75%.
